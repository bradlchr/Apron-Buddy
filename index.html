Frequently Asked Questions

\ \
\ How do I scan a recipe from a physical cookbook or magazine?\

Tap the + button in Apron Buddy and choose Scan Recipe Page. Point your camera at the page in good lighting and capture the photo. Apron Buddy uses on-device text recognition to extract the title, ingredients, and instructions into editable fields.

\
\ \
\ How do I import recipes from websites or PDFs?\

You can paste any recipe URL directly inside the app, use the Apron Buddy option from the Safari Share Sheet on iPhone, iPad, or Mac, or select Import PDF to parse digital cookbooks and recipe cards.

\
\ \
\ How does syncing work across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch?\

Apron Buddy syncs your recipes, weekly meal plans, and grocery lists automatically using your personal Apple iCloud account. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account on all devices and that iCloud Drive is enabled for Apron Buddy in your system settings.

\
\ \
\ How do I use Apron Buddy on Apple Watch?\

Open Apron Buddy on your Apple Watch to view your scheduled meals for the week, check off grocery items at the store, or follow step-by-step cooking instructions with built-in timers right from your wrist.

\
\ \
\ Can I export or back up my recipe library?\

Yes. Your recipes belong to you. Open Settings → Export Library inside the app at any time to create a portable backup of your entire recipe collection.

Privacy Policy

\

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

\ \

Apron Buddy is designed around a simple principle: your recipes, meal plans, and kitchen notes are personal and belong exclusively to you.

\ \
\
\ No Third-Party Tracking\ Zero ad networks, behavioral trackers, or data brokers.\
\
\ Private iCloud Sync\ Your library is stored locally on your devices and in your private iCloud container.\
\
\ On-Device Scanning\ Cookbook photos and PDFs are processed directly on your Apple device.\
\

Apron Buddy does not require you to create a proprietary developer account, and we do not operate external servers that store your personal recipes.

  • User Content (Recipes, Meal Plans & Lists): All recipes, photos, notes, meal schedules, and grocery items you add are stored locally on your device and synced across your Apple devices via Apple\'92s CloudKit / iCloud infrastructure, governed by Apple\'92s Privacy Policy.

  • Web Imports: When you import a recipe from a URL, your device fetches that webpage directly to extract the recipe text.
