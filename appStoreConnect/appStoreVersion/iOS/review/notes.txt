No Apron Buddy account or sign-in is required for the included sample recipes, recipe editing, pantry filtering, planning, cooking, or PDF export. Household sharing is optional and uses the Apple Accounts of two participants.

To try cooking, go to Recipes, open Maple oat pancakes, and tap Start cooking. Advance to Rest the batter to see inline measurements, the timer, and the ingredient peek in the top toolbar. The system requests alarm permission when needed. Swipe down to minimize the cooking sheet, scroll the recipe library, and tap the bubble between the active tab and Search to resume. Lock the phone to see the cooking Live Activity and, when a timer is running, its separate Live Activity.

Use the gear button to open Settings. Appearance contains the themes and alternate app icons. Household lets a signed-in iCloud user create a library, optionally copy their personal recipes and plans, and invite a second iCloud user. Both users can update the household catalog, meal plans, and grocery lists.

Chef’s Tip Jar is in Settings. The three consumable tip products are optional and unlock no features. The products are apronbuddy.tip.small, apronbuddy.tip.medium, and apronbuddy.tip.large.

Apple Intelligence is optional. Recipe import, ingredient matching, and meal planning have on-device fallbacks when it is unavailable.
