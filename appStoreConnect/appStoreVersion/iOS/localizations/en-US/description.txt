Keep family recipes, weeknight favorites, and new ideas in one kitchen. Apron Buddy helps you save recipes, decide what to cook, and follow each step on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch.

SAVE RECIPES FROM ANYWHERE
Scan a cookbook page or import from the web, a PDF, a document, or an image. Review the ingredients and instructions before saving. Organize recipes into cookbooks, rate favorites, and add your own photos and notes.

COOK STEP BY STEP
See measurements in the instructions, scale quantities for the people you're feeding, and group ingredients into sections like cake and frosting. Tap a time in the instructions to start a timer, or peek at the ingredient list without losing your place. On iPhone, resume cooking from the tab bar and check your step and timers in separate Live Activities. Follow steps and timers on Apple Watch.

MAKE A PLAN WITH WHAT YOU HAVE
Filter your saved recipes by up to three ingredients in your pantry. Use your favorites and cooking history to find something you haven't made in a while. Build a meal plan, combine ingredients into a grocery list with readable fractions, and send the list to Reminders.

SHARE A KITCHEN
Use iCloud to keep your recipes and cookbooks in sync across devices. Invite someone to a shared Household library with recipes, cookbooks, meal plans, and grocery lists. Both members can add recipes, edit plans, and check off groceries.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose a theme and alternate iPhone or iPad app icon. Print a recipe or export a styled PDF for a binder or to share. Apron Buddy includes an optional Chef's Tip Jar.
