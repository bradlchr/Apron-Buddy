Your Complete Kitchen Companion
Every recipe, synced across iPhone, iPad, Mac & Watch.

Keep family favorites, web finds, and weeknight staples together in a searchable recipe library. Apron Buddy helps you bring recipes in, make them your own, plan what to cook, and follow each step in the kitchen.

Scan Any Cookbook Page
Turn printed pages and magazines into digital recipes in seconds.
Capture one page or several with your camera. Apron Buddy reads the recipe so you can review and edit the ingredients and instructions before saving.

Clip From the Web & PDFs
Skip the clutter—save just the ingredients and steps.
Import a recipe from a website, PDF, text document, or recipe image. Batch import a folder of files, then review each recipe as it is added.

Cook Hands-Free
Follow steps and run timers right from your Apple Watch.
Use step-by-step cooking mode on iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Start recipe timers from the instructions and continue cooking with the paired Apple Watch.

Plan Meals in Minutes
Build your weekly menu and auto-generate your grocery list.
Make a meal plan around your schedule, special occasions, and food preferences. Start with your own recipes, choose how many people are eating, and plan for leftovers. Generate a grocery list grouped by category, keep it in Apron Buddy, or add items to a Reminders list.

MAKE YOUR RECIPE LIBRARY YOURS
Create cookbooks, search and filter recipes, and add recipes to a cookbook from the recipe card. Rate and favorite dishes, write personal notes, save photos of the finished meal, and print recipes when you want a paper copy. Share a recipe or cookbook with others through iCloud.

ADJUST AS YOU COOK
Scale ingredient amounts when servings change and convert between measurement units. Edit imported recipes so the saved version matches the way you cook.

YOUR APPLE DEVICES, IN SYNC
Use Apron Buddy on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch. With iCloud enabled, your recipes and cookbooks stay in sync across your devices, ready for planning or cooking.
